# Strategic-Sales-Insights-PowerBI
# Online Retail Analysis - Tata Virtual Internship 

## Project Overview
This project involves a strategic analysis of global sales data for an online retail store. The objective is to provide actionable insights for the CEO and CMO to support expansion and resource planning. 

## Data Preparation & Cleaning
To ensure the integrity of the analysis, a rigorous data scrubbing process was performed:
* **Negative Values:** All records containing negative quantities and unit prices were removed.
* **Data Validation:** The final dataset ensures that the reported **$10.67M total revenue** is based on valid, successful transactions.

## Tools Used
* **Power BI:** For data visualization and dashboard creation.
* **Power Query:** For data transformation and scrubbing.

---

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Revenue Trends & Seasonality
* **Observation:** Revenue remained stable from January to August, averaging **$712k** monthly.
* **Peak Period:** A significant surge occurred in Q4, with revenue peaking at **$1.5M** in both November and December.
* **Finding:** The business is highly seasonal, with the final quarter serving as the primary revenue driver.

### 2. International Growth Markets
* **Analysis:** Excluding the UK, the top revenue-generating countries are the **Netherlands, Ireland (EIRE), and Germany**.
* **Recommendation:** These regions represent the highest potential for continued international investment.

### 3. Customer Base Stability
* **Distribution:** The top revenue-generating customer accounts for **$280k**, only 7.7% more than the second-highest customer.
* **Conclusion:** The customer base is well-diversified, reducing the risk associated with reliance on single large accounts.

### 4. Geographic Expansion Opportunities
* **Current State:** Revenue is heavily concentrated in **Europe and Australia**.
* **Opportunity:** Regions such as **Asia, Africa, and the Americas** show lower demand, indicating significant untapped market potential for global expansion.
